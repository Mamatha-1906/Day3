Chevella Bus Accident — Telangana

Date: 3 November 2025 · Location: Mirzaguda, Chevella, Ranga Reddy District

What Happened

In the early hours of 3 November 2025, a Telangana State Road Transport Corporation (TGSRTC) bus collided head-on with a gravel-laden tipper lorry on the Hyderabad–Bijapur highway near Mirzaguda village in Chevella mandal, Ranga Reddy district.

The lorry, reportedly overloaded and speeding, lost control and crashed into the bus, causing its load of gravel to spill into the bus compartment.
